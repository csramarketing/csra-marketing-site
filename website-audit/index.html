What we check on the call

Six things decide whether a homeowner who finds you online ends up on your schedule. We go through every one, on your live site, with you watching.

How you show up on Google

Where you rank when a homeowner searches for a roofer near them, and what they see first.

Your reviews

How many you have, what they say, and whether Google shows them off next to your name.

Your site on a phone

Most homeowners find you on a phone. We check speed and layout there first, not on a big screen.

How easy it is to call

One tap should reach you. We trace the exact path a visitor takes from landing to ringing your phone.

Your service pages

Roof replacement, repairs, storm work. Each page should pull its own weight and book its own jobs.

Your service area

What an audit finding looks like

Sample findings from a typical roofing website review. Yours will name your site, your pages, and your numbers.

Your call button disappears on phones

What it costs you

Most of your visitors arrive on a phone. If the button is gone, so is the call, and the visitor moves to the next roofer on the list.

The fix

A call button that stays on screen on every page. About an afternoon of work.

Your reviews never show up in search

What it costs you

Homeowners pick the roofer with stars next to the name. Without them showing, your listing gets skipped even when you rank.

The fix

Connect your review profile the right way so the stars appear beside your listing.

One page tries to sell every service

What it costs you

Google cannot tell what the page is about, so it ranks for nothing, and homeowners land on a page that answers nothing.

The fix

One page per service, each written for the one search it should win.
