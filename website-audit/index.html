Why it matters

Small leaks in your online presence cost real roofing jobs

A replacement job is worth thousands. When your site is slow, your profile is thin, or a competitor outranks you, that job goes to another crew without you ever knowing your phone should have rung.

The first 3 results get the calls

Homeowners rarely scroll

When someone searches for a roofer, the top map results and the first websites collect nearly all the calls. If you sit on page two, you are invisible for that job no matter how good your work is.

Seconds decide who gets hired

Slow sites lose ready buyers

A homeowner with water coming through the ceiling will not wait on a slow page or hunt for a phone number. They hit back and call the next roofer. Speed and a clear call button are revenue features.

Trust is checked before the call

Reviews close the deal early

Strong recent reviews, real job photos, and a complete Google profile tell a homeowner you are safe to call. A thin profile with old reviews makes even a great roofer look like a gamble.
