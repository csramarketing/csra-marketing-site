Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

CSRA Marketing ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit csramarketing.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you reach out through our contact form or booking page, we collect the details you share with us: your name, business name, email address, phone number, and anything you write in your message. We use this only to respond to your inquiry and talk about working together.

We also collect basic analytics about how visitors use our site, such as which pages get viewed and how people found us. This comes from Google Analytics 4 and contains no names, emails, or other personal identifiers.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your messages and booking requests.
  • To prepare proposals and follow up on conversations you started.
  • To understand which pages help visitors most, so we can improve the site.

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We do not use it for anything you did not ask us to do.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to see aggregate traffic patterns on our site. GA4 sets cookies in your browser to do this. You can block cookies in your browser settings, and the site will still work for you.

Data sharing

We share your information only in these narrow cases:

  • With service providers who help us run the site (such as our hosting and analytics providers), who may only use it to provide their service to us.
  • If the law requires it, such as a valid court order.

We never sell your personal information to anyone.

Data retention

We keep inquiry details for as long as we need them to follow up with you and run our business, then we delete them. Analytics data is kept according to Google's retention settings.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Just email us and we will take care of it.

Children

Our site is for business owners. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be noted here.
