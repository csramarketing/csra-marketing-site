1 Send the day after the estimate A simple thank-you while your visit is still fresh in their mind.

Subject: Thanks for your time, [Homeowner First Name]

Hi [Homeowner First Name], Thanks for having us out to look at your roof. We appreciate you trusting Your Roofing Company with something as important as your home. I have attached your estimate again in case it got buried in your inbox. It covers everything we talked about during the visit, the materials, the work, and the cleanup, with no hidden costs added later. If anything in it is unclear, just reply to this email or give us a call. I am glad to walk through it line by line so you know exactly what you are paying for. Thanks again, [Your Name] Your Roofing Company [Your Phone Number]

2 Send 3 days after the estimate Answers the two questions almost every homeowner is quietly wondering about.

Subject: Two quick answers about your roofing estimate

Hi [Homeowner First Name], After an estimate, most homeowners ask us the same two questions, so I wanted to answer them here in case they are on your mind too. How long will the job take? Most roofs we do are finished in one to two days, and we leave your yard as clean as we found it. What happens if something goes wrong later? Every roof we install is backed by our workmanship warranty, on top of the manufacturer warranty on the materials. If a problem comes from our work, we come back and make it right. If you have other questions, big or small, just hit reply. There is no pressure at all. I would rather you feel sure about your decision than rushed into it. Best, [Your Name] Your Roofing Company [Your Phone Number]

3 Send 7 days after the estimate A gentle reminder that small roof problems rarely stay small.

Subject: Still thinking it over? That is completely fine

Hi [Homeowner First Name], A new roof is a big decision, and taking your time with it makes sense. I am not writing to push you. I just want to share one thing we see often in our line of work. Roof problems almost never fix themselves, and they rarely stay the same size. A small leak this month can quietly soak the wood under your shingles, and that turns a straightforward job into a bigger and more costly one. Catching it early is almost always the cheaper path. Your estimate from Your Roofing Company is still good, and we are here whenever you are ready. If it helps to talk it through on the phone for five minutes, I am happy to do that too. Take care, [Your Name] Your Roofing Company [Your Phone Number]

4 Send 14 days after the estimate Gives them a real reason to decide, plus an easier way to pay.

Subject: We can hold your estimate price a little longer

Hi [Homeowner First Name], Quick note from our side. The price on your estimate was based on the cost of materials when we visited, and those prices move around more than they used to. We can hold your price steady until [Date], so the number you saw is the number you pay. If paying for a roof all at once is the thing holding you back, ask us about our payment options. Plenty of our customers spread the cost out, and we are glad to explain how it works with no obligation. A simple yes or no reply is all I need. Either way, I appreciate you considering Your Roofing Company. Kind regards, [Your Name] Your Roofing Company [Your Phone Number]

5 Send 21 days after the estimate The polite closing email. It respectfully asks for a yes, a later, or a no.

Subject: Should we close out your file?

Hi [Homeowner First Name], I have sent a few notes about your roofing estimate and have not heard back, which is completely okay. I do not want to keep filling up your inbox, so this is my last email about it. If the timing is just not right, reply with the word LATER and I will check back in a few months. If you went with another company or changed your mind, reply with the word CLOSE and I will close your file with no hard feelings at all. And if you are ready to move forward, reply YES and we will get you on the schedule. Whatever you decide, thank you for letting Your Roofing Company take a look. If a friend or neighbor ever needs a roofer they can trust, we would be grateful for the referral. All the best, [Your Name] Your Roofing Company [Your Phone Number]

Why Following Up on Estimates Matters

Most estimates do not go cold because the homeowner said no. They go cold because life got busy. A steady follow-up fixes that.

Silence usually means busy, not no

Homeowners juggle work, kids, and a leaking ceiling at the same time. A friendly email at the right moment puts your company back at the top of the list.

The first roofer to follow up often wins

Plenty of roofing companies send an estimate and never say another word. Simply showing up again, politely, puts you ahead of most of your competition.

Follow-up builds trust before the job starts

Clear, patient emails show a homeowner exactly how you will communicate during the project. That confidence is often what tips a close decision your way.

It is the cheapest marketing you will ever do

These homeowners already know you and already need a roof. Winning one back costs a few emails, not another round of advertising. Pair it with steady roofing lead generation and your pipeline stays full on both ends.

Common Questions About Estimate Follow-Up

Quick answers before you start sending.

How many follow-up emails should a roofer send after an estimate?+

Five is a solid number. Most homeowners are busy, not uninterested. A short sequence spread over about three weeks keeps your company in front of them without feeling pushy, and the last email gives them an easy way to say yes, later, or no.

When should I send the first follow-up email?+

The day after you deliver the estimate. A quick thank-you while the visit is still fresh in their mind gets the most replies. After that, space the rest of the sequence a few days apart.

What should I change before I send these emails?+

Fill in the parts in brackets, like the homeowner's first name, your name, and your phone number. Everything else is ready to send as written. You can also paste in one detail from your visit, like the missing shingles you found, to make it feel even more personal.

Can I use these emails for repair estimates too?+

Yes. The sequence works for full roof replacements, repairs, and insurance work. Just adjust any line that mentions the type of job so it matches the estimate you sent.

Is this follow-up email generator really free?+

Yes. There is no sign-up and no cost. Type in your business name, copy the five emails, and send them to the homeowners who never got back to you.
