What your roofing website score checks

Mobile speed. How fast your site loads on a phone, and whether visitors wait or leave.

Lead generation. Whether a homeowner can call you or ask for an estimate without hunting for it.

Local SEO. Whether Google shows your site when nearby homeowners search for a roofer.

Trust signals. Whether your reviews, photos, and proof show up where a homeowner looks.

Accessibility. Whether everyone can use your site, including visitors who use a screen reader or keyboard.
